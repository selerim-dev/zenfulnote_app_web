Healing is often described like a project. Fix the wound. Become better. Move on.

But healing is not always about becoming someone new. Sometimes it is about reconnecting with what was already there before fear, shame, or survival took up so much space.

## You are not only what happened to you

Your wounds may shape your patterns, but they are not the whole of you.

There is creativity under the protection. There is tenderness under the defensiveness. There is wisdom under the part of you that learned to stay alert.

The work is not to erase the shadow. The work is to understand it well enough that it no longer has to run your life alone.

## Integration includes both sides

Wholeness does not mean becoming endlessly calm or positive. It means making room for the full range of your inner life.

Light and shadow both carry information. One shows what is possible. The other shows what still needs care.

<Callout>
  Your light is older than your wounds. The practice is remembering how to live from it again.
</Callout>

Start with one moment of awareness. Then another. Over time, those moments become a relationship with yourself.
